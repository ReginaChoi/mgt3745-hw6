## Trust Boundary

I sent the F-02 rows, Profile 3 from USERS.md, and the STYLE.md tokens to bolt.new and StackBlitz under their terms. I am accountable for that. None of it is real customer or personal data; every record I entered while testing is made up.

What came back in: bolt chose its own backend. It created a Supabase project, a Postgres table, and a Deno edge function, none of which are in TOOLS.md or ADR-001. It added `@supabase/supabase-js`, `react`, `lucide-react`, and Tailwind as dependencies. Its download included a `.env` with the Supabase project URL and the anon key, and its migration lets the anonymous role read, write, update, and delete the table.
